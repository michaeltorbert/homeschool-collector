# Broader concerns and recurring controls

Proposed; current concern passes are listing-specific and unsupported recurrence remains a program-level pass against the shown master schedule.

A one-off time/price/travel/format/duration/organizer problem is not a permanent constraint or topic dislike. Broadening requires an explicit dimension, value/window, child or household scope, effective dates and parent-chosen expiry/review date. No invented ninety-day expiry or repeating rule from one declined activity. Show which configured constraints are not checked.

Future recurring attendance preferences may apply at a confirmed program/series scope. A supported occurrence can have its own decision; unresolved masters cannot acquire invented occurrence IDs or mandatory sessions. Capture raw RRULE/RDATE/EXDATE and their provenance before claiming rule-only change detection; stored capture does not itself implement expansion/exceptions. Preserve timezone recurrence separately from event recurrence. Resolve supported multi-session/late-entry requirements from source evidence; otherwise unknown.

Keep append-only/idempotent constraint actions and independent bookmark/pass/source-review/reconsideration histories. Expiry removes only its configured effect and creates a local reason, not source publication or automatic Undo. Reassessment keys actual reference/effective dates. Material displayed schedule changes, including real parser corrections, can create timing reconsideration; harmless description/formatting edits cannot. Mixed general+timing stays in counted secondary access while pending critical notices remain visible.

Acceptance: one-off versus confirmed recurring window, expiry/edit/Undo/restart/replay, no topic penalty, timezone/DST boundaries, unknown recurrence and series identity, captured versus uncaptured rule-only changes, independent source/timing acknowledgements, mixed-reason prominence and preserved saved/hidden notices. Privacy: no private calendar/origin/notes in public fixtures or external extraction; deployment is separate.
