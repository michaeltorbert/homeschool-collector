# Future multi-subject and multi-operator projections

Deferred; current v1 accepts one local child, household and operator only. Stable labels are not authentication. A second active subject/operator is rejected until this design is implemented.

Keep personal preferences/fit child-scoped and common schedule/travel household-scoped, with explicit actor/selected-attendee context. One child's pass must not remove another child's opportunity. Each child needs New/Passed views; household bookmarks and creation-time notice relevance aggregate actual selected-attendee saved/surfaced interest without erasing prior pending notices. Any-child interest is not all-attendee provider eligibility: assess each participant and accompanying-adult role separately.

Multiple parents need actual hosted identity/permissions separately proved, append-only attribution and conflict presentation. Contradictory instructions must not silently become last-write-wins or apply to siblings; use the last nonconflicting explicit setting with an unresolved conflict label. Freeze scopes and explanation/reversal targets; no implicit cross-child negative learning or broad household inference.

Acceptance: migration preserves legacy one-child history; second-child isolation, any-child interest versus all-required-attendee rules, role/supervision uncertainty, conflicting-operator instructions, atomic scoped idempotency/Undo, concurrent review races and retained notice relevance. Define projections before lifting rejection guards. Hosted permission tests depend on the hosting issue; local synthetic IDs do not demonstrate authentication. No real family details in fixtures or public specs.
